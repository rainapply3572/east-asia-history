Historical territory polygons in territories.js are derived from:

  aourednik/historical-basemaps
  https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps

Upstream license: GNU General Public License v3.0 (see GPL-3.0.txt).
The license situation of the GeoJSON data is discussed by the upstream
project; this site treats the snapshots as GPL-3.0-licensed material.

Modifications made for this site:
  - Selected snapshots from 300 BCE to 2000 CE
  - Clipped to an East Asia bounding box (70°E–155°E, 5°N–56°N)
  - Simplified (Mapshaper, Visvalingam, keep-shapes) and rounded coordinates
  - Dropped unnamed leftover polygons; a few unnamed island polygons
    in Taiwan and the Ryukyus were labelled "Taiwan" / "Ryukyu" for display
  - Traditional Chinese labels were added for display only; original NAME
    values are unchanged

Boundaries are approximate reconstructions for continental-scale mapping
and are not suitable as evidence of precise historical frontiers.

Corresponding source for the modified data: territories.js in this folder,
generated by scripts/build_territories.py from the upstream GeoJSON files.
